Run Princess Run: privacy policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Run Princess Run, on the App Store as "Run Princess Run: Crown Chase", does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

  • The game has no accounts and asks for no name, email, location, photos or contacts.
  • It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party code.
  • It makes no connection to the internet.
  • It has no purchases and no links to websites or other apps.

The only thing the game saves is its own progress: which levels are finished, crowns and gems collected, outfits unlocked, and sound settings. That stays on your device. Deleting the app deletes it, and "Erase progress" in the grown-ups page of Settings clears it at any time.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to request, correct or delete beyond that.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change ships.
