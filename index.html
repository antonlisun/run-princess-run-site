Run Princess Run

A storybook runner for iPhone and iPad, on the App Store as "Run Princess Run: Crown Chase". This page is where to get help with it.

How to play

The princess runs by herself. Tap to jump, tap again in the air to jump a second time, and hold to float down under a parasol.

It is too hard

Turn on Easy mode: the gear on the first screen, or the turtle on the pause card. The game slows down and hearts never run out.

There is no sound

The game follows the silent switch. Turn the ringer on, turn the volume up, and check that music and sounds are on behind the gear on the first screen.

Starting again

Open the gear, tap "For grown-ups", answer the sum it asks, and choose "Erase progress". Deleting the game does the same.

Where is a dress, crown or friend found?

The same grown-ups page lists where every piece of the wardrobe comes from.
